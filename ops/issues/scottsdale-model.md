## 1. Subject line + preview text
- **Subject = the headline of the single most exciting local item**, almost always a *new place* or *big change to a familiar place*: "Kuza opening in North Scottsdale..finally!", "Second Costco coming to Scottsdale?", "$70M Lakefront Project coming to McCormick Ranch", "Indoor Playground Opens in…". Named place + named neighborhood. No "Tampa in 5"-style formula, no date, no issue number.
- Short (3–8 words), conversational punctuation ("..finally!", "?"). Sentence case or loose title case.
- **Preview text = "Plus…" second hook or the first fact of the lead story** ("Plus a major construction project has officially broken ground", "and our first community happy hour").

## 2. Opening
Identical every issue, then 1–3 personal lines, then "Let's dive in."
> "Good morning, Scottsdale. You're reading Scottsdale Scoop, your insider pass to what's fun, fresh, and worth checking out around town. Every issue features hand-picked events, local business spotlights, and hidden gems that make our city great."

Then a personal aside (traveling, birthday, "back from Utah") and/or a one-line table of contents ("We'll also share a new attraction for the kiddos this summer and a thrilling date idea"). No weather, no news recap.

## 3. Section order and names (consistent across issues)
1. **SCOTTSDALE SNAPSHOT** – 3 items (2–4), each with an H4 headline + one photo.
2. **TOGETHER WITH [SPONSOR]** – a native sponsor block written in the same first-person voice, with one link and often a subscriber offer/promo code. Sometimes a second one lower down. Followed by "Want to get your business in front of 35K+ locals? Click the button below and let's talk!"
3. **LOCAL BUSINESS OF THE WEEK** – one non-sponsored business, ~100 words, ends with the disclosure "Nothing in this section is sponsored…".
4. **DATE SPOT OF THE WEEK** (Wed issues) / **HAPPY HOUR OF THE WEEK** (Mon issue) – one venue, firsthand ("I stopped into…"), specific dishes and prices.
5. **WEEKLY EVENTS LIST** – grouped by day ("Wednesday, October 7"), one line each: `emoji Event | Venue | Time`, linked to the ticket page; 8–15 lines, ends "For a full list of events check out our Scottsdale Event Calendar here."
6. **THE GOOD LIFE** – the most expensive home sold in Scottsdale that week: address, price, one-line reaction, Realtor.com link.
7. **SHARE FEEDBACK** – a one-question beehiiv poll ("Did you like this week's issue?", "Have you ever gone to a bar/restaurant or event because you saw it in Scottsdale Scoop?").
8. **RESOURCE LIST 🌵** – Business Directory link, "Connect with me" (LinkedIn), "Advertise with us", merch. (Early issue had "THE BULLETIN": paid one-line shout-outs for local businesses/events.)

## 4. Story length and voice
- Snapshot items run **~60–130 words**: 2–3 short paragraphs. Paragraph 1 = the fact (who/what/where/when, often the street address or cross streets). Paragraph 2 = the useful detail (hours, price, what's on the menu, who signed leases). Last line = a personal one-liner ("Stoked for this!", "Figure I'd share since it's a fun family option.", "I'm curious to see what takes shape here").
- **First person, warm, casual**, like a friend texting you: "Good news for all the parents", "Technically Phoenix…but close enough", "hear me out!". Light emoji, exclamation points, occasional "lol"-level informality.
- Specifics everywhere: street addresses ("15689 North Hayden Road"), hours ("Tuesday through Saturday from 4 to 9 p.m."), prices ("Tickets are $15 per person"), square footage, tenant names.
- Every item links out to the business/organizer site or to the news source (KTAR, AZ Big Media, Phoenix New Times) – the link is put on a phrase inside the copy, not a "Source:" line.

## 5. What it leads with (the editorial yardstick)
Counting the 18 Snapshot items across the 6 issues: **restaurant/food openings and food happenings 8**, **family/free/fun events and attractions 5**, **development that changes a familiar place (Costco, Lakefront ×2) 3**, other "nice to know" (Michelin honor, free veteran dental care) 2. **Zero crime, zero partisan politics, zero hard civic-process stories** (council votes appear only as "approved" outcomes inside a development item). Tone is positive and practical: "what's new, what's fun, what's coming".

## 6. Events / food / business / development framing
- **Events**: in Snapshot only when big/free/family ("Did I mention it's free?!"), with date, time, price, what's there, one link "Reserve your free spot here." Everything else goes to the day-by-day list.
- **Food**: openings framed as "X just opened / is opening" + address + hours + 1–2 menu specifics. Firsthand dining is clearly firsthand.
- **Business**: one non-sponsored pick with the disclosure line. Sponsors are clearly labelled "TOGETHER WITH".
- **Development**: dollar figure + developer + address + what's going in + timeline ("first phase expected to finish next fall"), stated as reported.

## 7. Links, CTAs, images
- One link per item (two max), on natural phrases. Calendar CTA after the events list; directory CTA in the Resource List; sponsor CTA ("Get your tickets here"); advertiser CTA after the sponsor block.
- **One photo per Snapshot item, per sponsor block, per business/date spot, per Good Life home.** Header image at the top. Images are a big part of why it feels "fun".

## 8. Sponsor placement
Right after the Snapshot (prime spot), and sometimes a second one before The Good Life. Labelled "TOGETHER WITH [NAME]" or "PARTNER SPOTLIGHT"; copy is first-person endorsement with an offer code.

## 9. Close
No sign-off paragraph. Poll → Resource list (directory, connect, advertise, merch). The "Want to get your business in front of…" pitch appears mid-issue.

## How issue #1 of The Tampa Scoop follows the model, and where it differs from the generic playbook

| Element | Playbook (generic) | Scottsdale model | What issue #1 does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject | "Tampa in 5: [lead consequence] + [standout event]" | Headline of the most exciting item, conversational | **Model**: "Rays Game 2 tonight + Rreal Tacos opens today" |
| Preview | not specified | "Plus…" second hook | **Model**: "Plus: Tampa Theatre turns 100, dengue spraying this week and when early voting starts" |
| Opening | one sentence, earns the scroll | Fixed welcome paragraph + personal line + "Let's dive in." | **Model** (fixed welcome written for Tampa; personal line kept to facts: it's issue #1) |
| Snapshot | 2–4 items, ~55 words, Deep/Punchy/Straight order | 3 items, 60–130 words, interest order, photo each | **Model**: 4 items in rank order, 55–130 words. Tone labels kept for the Desk |
| Voice | "informed neighbor", dry humour | First person, warm, exclamation points | **Blend**: first person and warm, with no invented firsthand claims ("I tried…") because nothing was visited |
| Event picks | 4 picks: Free / Family / Date night / Wildcard | Day-by-day events list + calendar link | **Blend**: day-by-day list from the Events tab, with Free / Family / Date night tags on 3 of them. No Wildcard: nothing in the tab fit |
| Food | "Try this" module | Openings in Snapshot + Date Spot of the Week | **Model**: "NEW SPOTS TO TRY" (Rreal Tacos) + "DATE SPOT OF THE WEEK" (V Modern Italian, sourced, not firsthand) |
| Business | One useful directory business | Local Business of the Week + disclosure | **Skipped** (no business is Published in the directory yet) |
| Development | Its own module with approved/proposed labels | Folded into Snapshot | **Playbook kept**: a short "DEVELOPMENT WATCH" because Tampa has 3 live items this week. Status words are kept exactly as the sources give them |
| Sponsor | Clearly labelled slot | "TOGETHER WITH" right after Snapshot | **None** this issue. The slot sits after the Snapshot once there is one |
| Good Life | not in playbook | Most expensive home sold that week | **Not in #1**. Needs a sourced weekly "top sale" feed (Realtor.com / Hillsborough property appraiser). Suggested for the Saturday issue |
| Poll | not in playbook | One-question beehiiv poll | **Model**: one poll question for Justin to add in beehiiv ("What do you want more of?") |
| Close | Submit event / suggest business / share | Resource list | **Blend**: "Submit an event" + "Suggest a business" links, forward line, and "Nothing in this issue is sponsored." |
| Images | not specified | One per item | Image slots marked in the HTML; Justin adds his own or licensed photos (none were invented or scraped) |

**Rubric yardstick taken from the model** (used in `radar/method.md`): stories that tell readers about a *new place, a fun or free thing to do, or a visible change to a place they know* score highest on "Scoop fit". Hard politics, crime and process stories score low on fit even when they are big news. They only make the issue when they change what readers do this week (e.g. dengue spraying, early voting dates).
