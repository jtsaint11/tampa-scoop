# From radar to inbox: how each issue gets made

About 60 minutes per issue, done the day before it goes out.

| Radar email arrives | Build the issue | Issue goes out |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 7 a.m. | Mon | Tue (news + week setup) |
| Wed 7 a.m. | Wed | Thu (weekend planner) |
| Fri 7 a.m. | Fri | Sat (food + next week) |

## Once only: let the Desk create beehiiv drafts
Viktor → Integrations → beehiiv → set the **proxy post** action to **Auto**. Without this, **Send to beehiiv** fails. Copy beehiiv HTML would paste one locked block you can't edit, so don't use it as a workaround. The Desk only creates drafts; nothing sends until you send it in beehiiv.

## 1. Pick from the radar email (10 min)
Open "Tampa Scoop radar" in Gmail. The cards are ranked best first and grouped (This weekend / News & quick hits / Food). Pick enough to fill the 7 sections:
- 1 lead story for **Tampa Snapshot** (usually the top News card)
- 3 to 5 events for **Weekly Events List**
- 1 food or night-out pick for **Date Spot of the Week**
- 1 business for **Local Business of the Week**
- 1 easy, feel-good item for **The Good Life**

Open "Read source →" on each pick. Note anything in the "Check before printing" box; those details come through as VERIFY.

## 2. Put the picks into the Scoop Desk (2 min each)
Every radar card has a grey **Copy into Scoop Desk** block. Copy it, then in the Desk go to Story queue → **Add to the queue** → **Paste an intake block** → **Fill the form from this block** → glance at the form → add it to the queue.
The block already has the issue date, section, tone, facts from the source page, the source link and anything to check (Known gaps). Change the tone if you want a different feel.

## 3. Assemble the issue (30 to 40 min)
Issues → pick the date → **Open issue builder** → **Write sections**. For each of the 7 sections:
1. Tick the stories that belong to it, then **Save context**.
2. Optional: add a command ("lead with the free parking").
3. **Write drafts**, then **Approve** the best of Deep / Punchy / Straight.
4. Fill in every yellow VERIFY and fix anything in coral, then **Save**.

When all 7 show approved: Subject line box → **Regenerate** → **Use this** on the best pair.

## 4. Push to beehiiv (1 min)
Click **Send to beehiiv**. You get a draft in issue #1's layout with the Events Calendar and Business Directory links already in it.

## 5. Finish and send in beehiiv (10 to 15 min)
1. Open the draft in Posts.
2. At each yellow **PHOTO HERE** line, add your image in that spot and delete the line.
3. Read it through once. Small text edits are fine right in beehiiv.
4. Send yourself a **test email** and check it on your phone.
5. Publish to email and web, then either send now or schedule it for the morning of the issue day.

## After it's out
Nothing more to do. The next radar skips stories you've already used. You can Cut or Delete leftover stories in the Desk if the queue gets cluttered.
