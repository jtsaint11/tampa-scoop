# Share pack recipe (any issue)

**Inputs:** the sent issue's plain text, the signup link (tampascoop.beehiiv.com), events calendar and directory links, today's date.

**Step 1. Pick.** 1 food/new-spot item (best evergreen hook), 1 weekend event set, 1 "useful" item (scam, voting, closure). Mark each item FRESH (has a date) or EVERGREEN. Anything dated before today is out; make an evergreen version of every time-bound piece.

**Step 2. Write the pieces.**
| Piece | Length | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| IG/FB carousel | 5-7 slides, headline ≤6 words + ≤20 words each | Slide 1 = hook, last = "Tue news · Thu weekend · Sat food" + tampascoop.beehiiv.com. Caption ≤120 words, 5-8 hashtags |
| Reddit r/tampa | 100-250 words | Info first, bullet list, "Disclosure: I write…" + link once at the end. Check sub rules; drop link if not allowed |
| FB group / Nextdoor | 60-100 words | "Hi neighbors", 2-3 items, one link. Check group promo-day rules |
| Vertical video | 30-45s, 6-8 shots | Hook in 2s with a number or a "new" claim; on-screen text every shot; your own footage only; CTA "link in bio" |
| X / Threads | ≤280 chars | 2 facts + cadence + link |
Add a "When to post" line to each (weekend content Thu-Fri; food Sat; Reddit weekday mornings).

**Rules:** every name, date, price and address copied from the issue, never added. No "ultimate/game-changer", no em dashes, no fake reviews, no "DM me", no giveaways-for-follows, no posting the same text in many groups the same day. Always say Justin writes it. No stock or AI photos of real places. Cadence goal from the playbook: 4 short videos a week, events-first, "Get Thursday picks" as the main CTA.

**Slides:** 1080x1350, sand #FFF6EA background, ink #1D2B33 text, teal #0F5C6E headlines, coral #EE6C4D accent bar, gold #F7B538 for the CTA slide. Render with `render_slides.py` (edit the SLIDES list).
