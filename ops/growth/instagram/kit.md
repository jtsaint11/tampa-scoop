**Category:** Media/News Company (other good choices: Newspaper, Publisher).

**Setup steps**
1. Profile picture: upload `profile.png`. It's made to fit the circle crop.
2. Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → **Creator** (more flexible for one person and gives you full audio for Reels). Choose Business only if you plan to run ads through Meta Business Suite. Either one gives you insights.
3. Category: Media/News Company, and turn on "Display category on profile".
4. Contact options: add your email and turn the **Email button on**, so readers and businesses can send tips. Leave phone off.
5. Add the bio and the three links above.
6. Connect a Facebook Page later if you want to cross-post. You don't need it to start.

## 4. Two-week starter routine (about 30-45 minutes a day)
**Posts:** the 9 grid posts above (4-5 a week).
**Short videos (4 a week, per the plan):** Reels, cross-posted to TikTok/Shorts. Use your own footage only and keep captions on.
- Mon: "This week in Tampa in 30 seconds" (you on camera, read the 3 top items from Tuesday's issue)
- Wed: a neighborhood walk-through (you walking, with on-screen text)
- Thu: "3 weekend picks" from the Thursday planner, or the Rreal Tacos / V Modern Italian script from the issue #1 share pack
- Sat: a food spot from the Saturday issue (your plate, the outside, the price if the issue lists it)

**Stories (daily, 2-4 frames):**
- Issue days (Tue/Thu/Sat): a teaser frame, then a link sticker to subscribe. Save it to that day's highlight.
- Other days: a poll or question sticker ("Best brunch in Tampa?"), behind-the-scenes, or reshare a local account's post with credit.
- Turn the best comments and replies into story leads and future items.

**Weekly check (Sun, 10 min):** which post got the most saves and shares, which Reel got the most watch time. Do more of that next week.

## 5. Daily 10-account engagement routine (15 minutes)
Every day, find 10 local accounts and leave real, specific comments (not "🔥"). Follow accounts you actually want in your feed and don't unfollow to game it. Rotate through these types:
- Restaurants and cafés you've covered or plan to cover (comment on new-menu or opening posts)
- Event venues, theaters and organizers whose events show up on your calendar
- Neighborhood accounts and associations (Downtown, Hyde Park, Ybor City, Seminole Heights, and so on)
- Official city and county accounts (reshare useful notices with credit)
- Local food and things-to-do creators (comment on their posts)
- Chambers and business groups like the Tampa Bay Chamber, Ybor City Chamber and Tampa Downtown Partnership
- Local businesses that would fit the directory
Also: reply to every comment on your own posts within the first hour, and answer DMs every day.
Never buy followers, run follow/unfollow, or use engagement pods.
